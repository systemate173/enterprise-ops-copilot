# INCIDENT_SEVERITY

## Rough mapping
- High: outage, widespread inability to use core systems, login/VPN blocked, major customer impact
- Medium: intermittent failures, degraded performance, partial impact during peak hours
- Low: isolated issues, minor delays, workaround exists, low customer impact

## Notes
- If impact or scope is unclear, treat as higher until confirmed
- If incident affects access/authentication, prioritize quickly due to productivity + security implications
